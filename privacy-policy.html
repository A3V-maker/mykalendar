In short: the birthdays, anniversaries, notes and everything else you enter stay on your phone. My Kalendar has no accounts and does not sell your data. The app shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

My Kalendar (the "app") is published by [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS, COUNTRY] ("we", "us"). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]. For people in the EU/UK, we are the controller for the limited data described in sections 3 to 5.

2. Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following on your device only. We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded to us:

  • names, dates, notes, phone numbers and email addresses you type in for the people and events you track;
  • reminders, gift records, milestone entries, photo captions and your settings (language, traditions, country, display choices);
  • scheduled notifications for your alerts.

Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, removes it. You can also export a JSON backup with Settings → Backup. The backup goes wherever you choose to share it, so keep it somewhere safe.

3. Device permissions

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature and is optional:

  • Contacts: to import names and birthdays you pick. Contact data is read on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • Calendar: to add events to your phone calendar when you ask. Nothing is read back or sent to us.
  • Notifications: to show your alerts.

5. Why we use it and our legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Settings file and keeping the app working and secure: our legitimate interest in operating the app.
  • Device permissions: your consent, given in the system prompt.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Your own data: you can view, edit, export and delete it in the app at any time. Because it stays on your phone, we hold no copy to access or erase.
  • Rights: under GDPR/UK GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and object to processing, and you may complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • US state privacy laws (e.g., California): we do not sell personal information.

7. Retention and transfers

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Providers keep data under their own policies.

8. Children

My Kalendar is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data to a provider through the app, contact us.

9. Security

Your data lives in your phone's app storage and is protected by your device's own security. Network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
